# Famous Soccer Player Celebrations — Reference Review

Reference images for **8 unique, iconic soccer player celebrations** to draw for the game (excluding players already in the mode and drill cards: Henry, Messi, Son, Ronaldo, Del Piero, Suárez, Rashford, Haaland, Mbappé, Griezmann, Dybala, Bellingham).

---

## 1. Jamie Vardy — Corner Flag Slide

- **Key Pose / Motion:** Aggressive slide tackle into the corner flag, snapping the pole with one arm raised.

![Jamie Vardy — Corner Flag Slide](./images/15_vardy_corner_flag.jpg)

---

## 2. Christian Pulisic — Arm Over Eyes (AC Milan)

- **Key Pose / Motion:** Standing upright, left forearm held horizontally across eyes/forehead with fist clenched.

![Christian Pulisic — Arm Over Eyes (AC Milan)](./images/09_pulisic_arm_over_eyes.jpg)

---

## 3. Gareth Bale — Heart Hands

- **Key Pose / Motion:** Standing upright facing the crowd, both hands forming a heart in front of the chest.

![Gareth Bale — Heart Hands](./images/16_bale_heart.jpg)

---

## 4. Wayne Rooney — "Knockout" Boxing

- **Key Pose / Motion:** Wide boxing stance throwing shadow punches (followed by falling flat on back).

![Wayne Rooney — "Knockout" Boxing](./images/17_rooney_knockout.jpg)

---

## 5. Mohamed Salah — Yoga Tree Pose

- **Key Pose / Motion:** Balanced on one leg with opposite foot tucked to inner thigh, palms pressed together at chest.

![Mohamed Salah — Yoga Tree Pose](./images/11_salah_tree_pose.jpg)

---

## 6. Paul Pogba — "The Dab"

- **Key Pose / Motion:** Head tucked into bent elbow while opposite arm extends diagonally upward.

![Paul Pogba — "The Dab"](./images/12_pogba_dab.jpg)

---

## 7. Didier Drogba — Double Knee Slide

- **Key Pose / Motion:** Forward turf slide on both knees, torso leaned back, fists clenched at sides.

![Didier Drogba — Double Knee Slide](./images/06_drogba_knee_slide.jpg)

---

## 8. Cole Palmer — "Cold Palmer" Shiver

- **Key Pose / Motion:** Arms crossed tightly across chest/shoulders, rubbing upper arms as if shivering.

![Cole Palmer — "Cold Palmer" Shiver](./images/08_palmer_cold.jpg)
