WITH THE BALL

  • Hold a shot key to charge, release to strike. The longer you hold, the higher it goes — hold too long and it sails over the bar. The power shot takes longer to charge but is far harder.
  • The chip shot scoops a lofted shot over a keeper who has come off his line. Charge it quickly: too soft and he plucks it down, too hard and it clears the bar.
  • Hold up / down while shooting to pick a corner.
  • Tap the normal shot key for a fake shot: defenders may bite, and you get a burst of speed. Tapping any other shot key fires a quick, weak shot.
  • Hold a shot key as a loose ball arrives for a first-time shot: the same button gives a volley, half-volley, header or diving header depending on how the ball arrives, and a quick shot at speed is driven; shooting across the body with your weaker foot uses the outside of the strong foot. No extra buttons. Press a skill key while charging to cancel the shot.
  • Sprinting knocks the ball further ahead — a defender can nick it. Hold Jockey with the ball for close control: slower, tighter touches, and the ball is shielded when a defender is near (it drains stamina). Tap Jockey at speed to stop, then Sprint + a direction right after to burst away (stop-and-go).
  • Pass (2v2) goes to your teammate automatically.
  • Pitch (offline, before kick-off): Firm is fast and bouncy, Lush is heavy and holds the ball up, Astro has true bounce where spin bites. Rain makes any pitch slick. Struck balls carry topspin or backspin (a chip dies on landing, a driven shot skids on) and the goal frame is a round tube: crossbar and posts rebound the ball realistically. Taller, stronger players win more physical challenges, and a harder, faster challenge hits harder.
  • Land a skill move on a defender up close and you can break their ankles: they hit the floor and you're gone.

DEFENDING

  • Tackle, slide tackle, shoulder (a barge that can knock the ball loose) and focus (hold for a low side-on stance: you can read a move and steal the ball mid-skill) have their own keys, and may share keys with attacking moves — you are only ever one or the other.
  • Jockey to face the ball and shuffle; tackles from a jockey are stronger. Stand in the way to block shots.

RULES

  • Goal at the top. Each rep starts with the attacker (with the ball) on the spot 10 yards outside the 18-yard box, in the centre, and the defender standing in front of them. First to three goals wins.
  • After a shot, win the rebound and clear it outside the box. Play then carries on and the clearer becomes the attacker. Only the attacker can shoot: a defender who wins the ball has to get it outside the box before shooting. If the keeper catches the ball they throw it back out of the box for either player to win. After a goal, the scorer starts with the ball.
  • No fouls or restarts: a ball that goes out of play is given to whoever was defending.
  • Drills: one player against the keeper, paying credits for your score. The Testing Arena is free practice with every skill move and no score limit. Drill shots are taken from the spot: aim with the direction keys or the mouse. The keeper level sets how good the keeper is.

CELEBRATIONS

After you score your player celebrates. Every player has a signature celebration; in Store > Celebrations you can buy others (fist pump, arms wide, point up, salute, robot, sit down, knee drop, spin leap) and equip one for all your players, or go back to the signature one. They are cosmetic: they never change a match, they play in 2D and 3D (3D players with a sprite sheet keep their default 2D pose), and online your opponent sees yours. The 3D goal camera frames each one differently.

SKILL MOVES

Every skill move is unlocked. Moves are chords of J K L: press them together (within a split second) with the ball at your feet. Hold Sprint with a single key for the other three. Steer to choose the exit direction. Your player needs enough skill stars for a move, otherwise the input does a simpler one. Tackles fail against a well-timed move and land easily on a badly timed one.
