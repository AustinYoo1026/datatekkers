CONTROLS

KEYS

Gamepads work too (stick, A shoot, B finesse, Y power shot, right stick click chip, X stepovers, RT sprint, LT jockey).

WITH THE BALL

  • Hold a shot key to charge, release to strike. The longer you hold, the higher it goes — hold too long and it sails over the bar. The power shot key takes longer to charge but is far harder.
  • The chip shot key scoops a lofted shot over a keeper who has come off his line. Charge it quickly: too soft and he plucks it down, too hard and it clears the bar.
  • Land a skill move on a defender up close and you can break their ankles — they hit the floor, and you're gone.
  • Tap a shot key with the ball for a fake shot: defenders may bite.
  • Hold up / down while shooting to pick a corner.
  • Hold a shot key as a loose ball arrives for a first-time shot.
  • Press a skill key while charging to cancel the shot.
  • Sprinting knocks the ball further ahead — a defender can nick it.
  • Jockey with the ball to shield it from the defender.

WITHOUT THE BALL

  • Space — standing tackle. Get close; a tap also pokes at a loose ball.
  • Sprint + direction + Space — sliding tackle. Long reach, slow recovery.
  • Jockey to face the ball and shuffle; tackles from a jockey are stronger.
  • Stand in the way to block shots.

SKILL MOVES

Skill moves are chords of J K L: press them together (within a split second) with the ball at your feet. Hold Sprint with a single key for the other three. Steer with the stick to choose the exit direction. Only Body feint and Stepovers are free: buy the rest in the Store. In a match, a move you have not unlocked — or one your player lacks the skill stars for — becomes a simpler one; the testing arena unlocks every move. Tackles fail against a well-timed move — and land easily on a badly timed one.

RULES

  • Goal at the top. Each rep starts with the attacker (with the ball) on the spot 10 yards outside the 18-yard box, in the centre, and the defender standing in front of them. First to three goals wins.
  • After a shot, win the rebound and clear it outside the box. Play then carries on and the clearer becomes the attacker. Only the attacker can shoot: a defender who wins the ball has to get it outside the box before shooting. If the keeper catches the ball they throw it back out of the box for either player to win. After a goal, the scorer starts with the ball.
  • No fouls or restarts: a ball that goes out of play is given to whoever was defending.
  • Drills: one player against the keeper. Penalty Shootout (5 kicks), Target Practice (hit the lit corner for 3 points) and Chip Challenge (lob a keeper who has come off his line) pay credits for your score; the Testing Arena is free practice with every skill move unlocked and no score limit. You take drill shots from the spot — aim with the direction keys or the mouse. The keeper level sets how good the keeper is.
