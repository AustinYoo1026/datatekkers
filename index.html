In 1-player mode the arrow keys also move your player. Gamepads work too (stick, A shoot, B finesse, Y chip, X skill, RT sprint, LT jockey).

WITH THE BALL

  • Hold a shot key to charge, release to strike. Past the marker a normal shot becomes a power shot — faster, less accurate.
  • Hold up / down while shooting to pick a corner.
  • Hold a shot key as a loose ball arrives for a first-time shot.
  • Tap the skill key while charging to cancel — a fake shot.
  • Sprinting knocks the ball further ahead — a defender can nick it.
  • Jockey with the ball to shield it from the defender.

WITHOUT THE BALL

  • Space — standing tackle. Get close; a tap also pokes at a loose ball.
  • Sprint + direction + Space — sliding tackle. Long reach, slow recovery.
  • Jockey to face the ball and shuffle; tackles from a jockey are stronger.
  • Stand in the way to block shots.

SKILL MOVES

Press the skill key with a direction relative to where your player is facing (a diagonal counts as sideways). Holding Sprint selects the advanced move. Players without enough skill stars perform a simpler move instead. Tackles fail against a well-timed move — and land easily on a badly timed one.

RULES

  • First to five goals wins. The player who concedes kicks off.
  • No fouls, corners or throw-ins: a ball over the touchline is a kick-in for the other player; over the goal line it goes to the keeper.
