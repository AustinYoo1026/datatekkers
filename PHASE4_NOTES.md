## M3 Tournament (4.2): no sim change

- `js/cup.js` (pure, node-loadable; added to `index.html` after `ai.js` and to the require line of `tools/check.js`). `ISO.CUPS` is a table: Rookie Cup (ovr <= 78, easy / normal / normal, prizes 250 / 500 / 1500), Pro Cup (76-88, normal / normal / hard, 400 / 900 / 2500, needs the Rookie Cup won), Legends Cup (era `legend`, normal / hard / hard, 600 / 1500 / 4000, needs the Pro Cup), Street Cup (added in M4, any player, `rules: 'street'`, normal / normal / hard, 350 / 800 / 2200, needs the Rookie Cup). A 16-entrant cup or a league is a table row (`size`, one more `rounds` / `prize` entry): the bracket code works for any power-of-two field.
- `seedField(cup, meId, seed)`: you plus seven unique picks from everyone who fits the filter, then a seeded shuffle for the bracket slots. Local mulberry32 PRNG, never `Math.random`. The same seed gives the same bracket (asserted).
- State `{ id, seed, me, field[8], round, res[][], live }`. `res[r][f] = { a, b, w, sa, sb }`. The other fixtures of a round are only pending once your own match is recorded (`Cup.mineDone`), and each is simulated from `mix(seed, round, fixture)`, so a round interrupted by a reload resumes with identical results.
- `simMatch(a, b, seed, diff, rules)`: headless `Game.create` + two `ISO.AI`, to the end or 600 s, then the score, then the rating, then a seeded coin. Measured cost: about 30 ms a fixture on average (max 58 ms over 12 normal fixtures; Street rules 40 ms average, max 82 ms), well under the 150 ms threshold, so the real sim is used. Safety net kept: `ISO.CupFast = true`, or two consecutive fixtures over 150 ms, switches to a rating-weighted seeded roll (`how: 'roll'`).
- Profile (SCHEMA 3, was 2): `cup`, `cups: { id: { won, best } }`, `stats.cupWins`, `stats.streetWins`. Migration keys on shape: `Cup.valid` drops a cup whose id, players, field, round or results are unknown or inconsistent (asserted for nine corruptions); an unknown id in `cups` is dropped; a cup with `live: true` is dropped (elimination). `cupKickoff()` writes `live = true` and saves before the match; `recordCup(won, gf, ga)` clears it, pays the round prize on a win (credits, `rec.best`), and on the final win increments `cups[id].won` and `stats.cupWins` and clears the run; a loss or forfeit ends the run. The prizes are on top of the normal match credits (`recordMatch` with `mode: 'cup'`: no opponent-gap multiplier).
- Achievements: family `cups` (1 / 5 / 20 wins, the 5-win tier hands over `zanetti`, a Legend, so the Legends Cup is reachable) and family `street` (1 / 10 / 40 Street wins).
- UI: `js/cup-ui.js` (new file to avoid conflicts in `meta-ui.js`; hooked by one line in `ISO.Meta.onShow` and one in `renderPlay`). A Tournament card on the Play hub (the grid became 3 x 2 for six cards, smaller type), a `#cup` screen (cup list with requirements and prizes, your-player chooser limited to owned eligible players, the bracket, the PLAY QUARTERFINAL button, FORFEIT CUP, the sim animation one fixture every 10 frames). `App.mode = 'cup'` goes through `startMatch` (opponent from the bracket, `matchDiff` from the round, `matchWin` 3; restart is disabled and the pause menu says FORFEIT CUP; leaving the match forfeits). `showEnd` has the cup branch: through to the next round / eliminated / champion, with the prize.
- Phase 3 personalities: `buildMatch` already passes `{ pers: ISO.AI.profileOf(defs[i]) }` (wired by the Phase 3 session), so cup and street matches get the opponent's personality with no change here. The headless bracket fixtures use the plain brain (cheap and neutral).

## M4 Street Football (4.1)

- `ISO.RULES.street = { top 1.05, acc 1.15, skCost 0.6, skCd 0.7, stars 1, wallE 0.9, win 5, limit 180, outBox 2, wallGoal 2 }` in `js/data.js`.
- `js/game.js`: `Game.create({ rules: 'street' })` (ignored for drills, the arena and unknown ids) sets `G.rules`, `G.walls = true`, `G.win`, `G.limit`, and scales each player's `top` and `jog`. `RL(G)` resolves the table per call (`NORULES` when absent: every multiplier exactly 1, `stars` 0). Hooks: the acceleration `rate` line (`acc`), skill stamina cost (`skCost`) and the two skill cooldowns (`skCd`), `pickSkill` stars (`min(5, stars + 1)`), wall restitution in the walls branch and the back board (`wallE`), `ball.wallHit` set when a side or end wall (or the back wall) is hit at more than 80 px/s, cleared in `resetBall`, on a new strike and when an outfielder takes possession, `goal()` points (`max(1, outBox if the goal's shot was struck from outside the box by the scoring team, wallGoal if wallHit)`), the clock in `step` (the leader wins at the limit; level score: `G.sudden = true` and the next goal wins).
- Fields that exist only with the rule set: `G.rules`, `G.limit`, `G.sudden`, `ball.wallHit`, and `pts` on the `goal` event and the `G.goals` entry. The default snapshot keys, events and bytes are unchanged.
- `setDef` (the penalty taker swap) is not hooked: rule sets are never used with drills.
- Decisions: the `street` map entry keeps no walls (giving it walls would change classic play for anyone who has it equipped); the sim forces walls only under the rules. A rules match whose equipped map has no walls is drawn on the wire-fenced `fs_roof` cage for the match (look only, nothing bought; `App.mapTmp` restores the equipped map on leaving to a menu screen). It works in 2D and 3D (the 3D stadium is built from `R.mapId()` and `G.walls`).
- Rain, pitch type and the 3D view all combine with the street rules (independent opts; walls plus rain is allowed, the pitch type only changes the surface table). Street Football is offline CPU only (the segment is hidden online and in drills).
- UI: a RULES segment (STANDARD / STREET) on the select screen (`App.rules`, actions `rules-std` / `rules-street`), a Street Football Play card (`mode-street`), the HUD clock counts down and shows SUDDEN DEATH, `GOAL! +2` banner (`render.js overlays`, shared with 3D) and a floating `+2` text in `render.js` and `js/3d/fx3d.js`. Rewards: `stats.streetWins`, family `street`.
- AI is unchanged: it reads `G.win` implicitly (the cap and clock need no AI change). Skipped as asked: the optional small-cage bounds.
- Soak (`node tools/sim.js 60 normal --rules=street`): NaN 0, stuck 0, unfinished 0, average 1.8 min a match, about 10% of goals are worth 2, sudden death in 2 of 60 matches, longest match 246 s.

## Tests

`tools/check.js` (60 matches plus scripted): same seed same bracket, different seed different bracket, every cup yields eight unique eligible entrants over 25 seeds, advance reaches `next,next,champion` with 4 / 2 / 1 fixtures, a loss eliminates, `simMatch` deterministic and the roll fallback, nine corrupt-cup migrations, `live: true` dropped on reload, cups record repair, the prize and unlock flow through the profile, the street rule fields absent by default and present when on, a plain goal 1, a wall-assisted goal 2, an outside-the-box goal 2, `wallHit` meaning nothing without the rules, a real wall bounce setting the flag, walls softer under street rules, the leader winning at the limit, a level score going to sudden death and the next goal winning, a default match not ending on the clock. `server/test-integrity.js`: a hostile client's `rules`, `win`, `limit`, `walls` (in the create message and the pick) change nothing, `server.js` never passes them, a pick and an input cannot carry rules.

## Known limits / needs a browser look

- Not run in a browser (no browser here): the Play hub with six cards, the `#cup` screen layout (bracket, list, chooser at 1280x720), the end screen text for the cup branch, the street HUD clock, the +2 banner and floating text in 2D and 3D, and `fs_roof` as the street look in 2D and 3D.
- A match that is won but whose end screen has not appeared yet (0.6 s) and is then reloaded counts as elimination (the `live` flag is still set).
- Wall bonus is "a wall was hit (over 80 px/s) since the last strike, restart or outfielder possession". A deflection off a body or a keeper (no possession) keeps it, so a wall bounce followed by a keeper parry that goes in still counts. A pass (no strike flag) does not clear it either.

## M5 Training Arena sandbox (4.3)

- Sim (`js/game.js`): `Game.create({ solo: true, sand })`. `sandOpts` clamps everything: `{ defenders 0-3, defMode 'static'|'cpu', keeper 'live'|'dummy'|'none', nerf -10..30, infStam, spot {x 40..FW-70, y 60..FH-60}, ball 'feet'|'rolling'|'high', rain, turf }` and sets `G.sand` (and `G.sandT`) ONLY when passed (a match, a built-in drill or the plain arena has no such field, asserted). The arena stays solo-like (win 1e9, only you score: `goal()` forces team 0 under `G.sand`), but team 1 is now updated and collided. Players are `[you, defenders...]` (team = index > 0 ? 1 : 0, like the free-kick wall). Hooks, all no-ops when `G.sand` is absent: the `!(G.solo && p.team === 1)` update skip, `collideBodies`, the keeper update (keeper `none`: parked at -900), `infStam` pins stamina to 100 after `updatePlayer`, `sandPlace` in `setupRep` (spot, defenders at fixed offsets in front of you, dummy / none keeper, rolling or dropped ball), `sandStep` in place of `drillCheck` (a defender holding the ball for more than 0.5 s calls `endRep(G, 'lost', 0)`; any out already ends the rep, and `setupRep` forces you to attack). Sandbox rain and pitch type are applied from `G.sand`.
- App (`js/arena-ui.js`, new, hooked by one-line calls in `main.js`: `ISO.ArenaUI.simOpts / roster / fill / timeScale / onEvent / frame`): an in-match DOM panel over the HUD (right margin of the pitch, collapsible): defenders 0-3 and STATIC / CPU, keeper LIVE / DUMMY / NONE and EASY / NORMAL / HARD, START SPOT (five presets, plus PICK ON PITCH: the next click on the 2D pitch, through the `render.js` transform), ball (feet / rolling / drop), INF STAMINA, RAIN, pitch type, speed 1x / 1/2x / 1/4x (an App-side time scale in the accumulator, like `Pres.timeScale`; no restart), RESET REP, CLEAR SESSION, and links to the Penalty and Free Kick drills. Any change but speed restarts through `startMatch()` with the new opts. CPU defenders are driven by `ISO.AI.update` (`ArenaUI.fill`, after the two seats in `inputs()`).
- Accuracy: app-side from `kick` events (with a `shot` type, by you), `goal`, catch saves and outs, by shot type, plus `G.stats.onTarget`. Shown as session vs all-time (shots, goals and rate, on target, best streak, per type). Persisted as `P.arena = { shots, goals, onTarget, byType: { type: { s, g } }, bestStreak }` (`ISO.DrillSpec.normArena` in `migrate`: clamped, back-filled by shape; `addArena` folds a session delta; flushed every 15 s, on leaving the match and on `pagehide`).
- Economy: the arena's 2 CR per goal is paid only against a live keeper at easy level or harder (`nerf <= 16`), so a keeper-less sandbox cannot be farmed. Skills and the stars unlock are the arena's already.
- Cut: wind (no wind exists in the sim), practising individual skills beyond the arena's all-moves unlock (the skill chord is the existing arena).

## M6 Custom Drill Creator (4.4)

- `js/drillspec.js` (new, pure, node-loadable, loaded before `profile.js`): `ISO.DrillSpec`. Schema v1 `{ v, id, name, total 1-20, time 3-30, start {x,y,jx,jy}, keeper {mode,x,y,nerf}, defenders (<= 4) [{x,y,mode:'static',face}], cones (<= 12) [{x,y,r 4-14}], walls (<= 6) [{x0,y0,x1,y1,h 10-80}], targets (<= 4) [0-3 goal-mouth zones], score {goal 1-5, bull 1-5, types {normal|power|finesse|chip: 0-5}} }`. `validate(obj)` returns a clamped, normalised COPY or `null`; never throws; a present but non-finite or non-number field rejects; an object overlapping your start (or its jitter box) rejects; idempotent (asserted). Also `toCode` / `fromCode` (`ISOD1:` + base64 JSON, always through `validate`, 8000 char cap), `loadSlots / putSlot / delSlot` (key `isofootball.drills.v1`, separate from the profile, 20 slots, every access in try/catch, every read validated), `credits`, `normArena / addArena`.
- Sim: `opts.drill: 'custom'` with `opts.drillDef` (validated again at `create`; invalid or missing = no drill) makes `makeDrill('custom')` with `G.drill.def` (plain data) and sets `G.cust`. `DRILLS.custom` is the only addition to the built-in table. A dribbling drill like One on One (`D.dribble`, set for `oneonone` and `custom`; the old `kind === 'oneonone'` tests became `D.dribble`, same behaviour). Per rep: your start plus the authored jitter (`G.rand`, custom only), the keeper (live, dummy or none; its level is part of the drill), up to four stationary defenders (the free-kick wall mechanism), a rep clock from the whistle (`def.time`, miss at the limit), goal worth `score.goal`, `score.bull` in a lit zone, a shot type in `score.types` replacing the goal's worth (0 = does not count). Cones: static circles for you (`custPlayer`) and vertical capsules for the ball (`hitSeg` got an optional radius, default `FRAME.r`: the goal frame call sites are unchanged). Walls: a new `hitPanel` reflect helper (thin vertical panel, restitution 0.6, the ball clears a wall lower than the ball's height), and you cannot walk through them. `custBall` / `custPlayer` run only when `G.cust` is set.
- Editor (`js/editor-ui.js`, new; `<section id="editor">` in `index.html`, `'editor'` added to `MENU_SCREENS`, a Drill Creator card on the Drills screen): top-down canvas with the `render.js` pitch transform (`screen = (OX + Z * simY, OY + Z * (FW - simX))`), palette (cone, wall, defender; start and keeper are always there), drag, wall end handles, defender rotation dot or R / Shift+R / buttons, Delete, undo / redo (JSON snapshots, cap 100, Ctrl/Cmd+Z, Ctrl+Y), goal-mouth target zones (click on the canvas or the buttons), keeper mode and level, reps, seconds per rep, start spread, scoring, 20 save slots (load, play, delete), SAVE, SAVE AS NEW, RESET TO SAVED (the slot's version), NEW, TEST (validates, plays the drill through `startMatch()`, and CHANGE DRILL / CHANGE PLAYERS in the pause and end screens return to the editor), EXPORT / IMPORT code (an imported drill gets a fresh id and is not saved until you save it). The working draft may be invalid mid-edit; everything that leaves the editor goes through `validate`. The same `drawWorld` draws cones, walls and lit zones in the editor and in the match (one hook line in `render.js draw2D`). A custom drill is always drawn by the 2D view (`use3d` is false while `G.cust` is set).
- Credits: `recordDrill('custom:' + id, ...)` pays 1 CR per point, at most 8 CR a run (`DrillSpec.CR_PER_POINT`, `CR_CAP`), no win bonus, no `drillRuns` count (no achievement farming), best score kept per id; at most 30 authored-drill keys are kept in `P.drills`.
