# Phase 3 session 1: AI personalities (3.1), tactical AI (3.3, partly), AI testing (3.5)

Scope: offline CPU only. The server never runs an AI, so nothing here touches netcode, snapshots or `ISO.SIM_REV`. `ISO.AI.create(diff)` with no options is the pre-Phase-3 brain byte for byte (tripwire: section L of `tools/ai-test.js`, plus `check.js 60`, `sim.js 40/150`, `balance --smoke`, `anim-gate`, `test-determinism`, `bench-bandwidth` all unchanged).

## Architecture (`js/ai.js`)
- `ISO.AI.KNOBS = { key: [neutral, 'mul'|'add', lo, hi] }` (same shape as `ISO.MODS`); `NEUTRAL` is every default. Neutral multiplies by exactly 1 / adds 0, and every new `G.rand` draw sits behind a non-neutral knob test. No `Math.random`.
- `ISO.AI.ARCH`: seven archetypes `{ name, desc, knobs }`: aggressive, technical, defensive, clinical, unpredictable, counter, possession. `ISO.AI.archProfile(id)` gives a pure archetype table (tests).
- `ISO.AI.profileOf(def)` returns a frozen `{ id, name, desc, z }`, cached in a WeakMap on `def.base || def` (Prime and skins resolve like the base). `ISO.PERS_OFF` or `ISO.STYLE_OFF` returns neutral.
- `create(diff, { pers, lvl, adapt })`: `m.D` is the DIFFS entry itself, `m.pz` the personality table. `lvl` and `adapt` are accepted and ignored (session 2). AI memory (`mood`, `ctrT`, `holdT`, `possT`, `oppHad`, `hadBall`, `moods`, `dbg`) lives on the AI object only, never on G or players.
- Guardrails at the point of use: tackleP <= 0.9, slideP <= 0.25, skillP <= 0.6, eager 0.25..1.8. No archetype touches speed, think or foolP (decisions change, reactions do not).

## Knobs and hooks
Multiplicative: `tackle`, `slide` (tackle / slide chance), `pressGap` (goal-side gaps 22/50/95), `jockeyR` (the 95 px jockey radius), `skill`, `sg` (skill and stop-and-go rate), `range`, `eager`, `closeIn` (the 105 px always-shoot distance), `recover` (sprint stamina threshold 15/recover). Additive: `aimSmart` (added to D.smart for aim only), `minQ` (a shot is skipped while `shotQ < minQ`, which decays linearly to 0 between 8 and 12 s of continuous possession), `patience` (seconds of hold-up with `jockey` and drift to the open side when a defender is goal-side inside 110 px and shotQ is low), `counter` (seconds after winning the ball from the opponent: lane straight at goal, sprint threshold -8, eager x1.5, skill x0.5, shoot-now band 230 px, always-shoot x1.3), `dropDeep` (defender target lerps to the ball-to-goal line 160 px out when the ball is > 200 px away), `vary` (every 0.5 s, probability vary*0.25: borrow a random archetype's table for 1.5-3 s). `ISO.AI.shotQ` is a pure 0..1 function (distance over range, angle, blocker, pressure, keeper offset).
Moved to session 2: `laneBlock`, `ctxSkill`, `rebound`.

## Derivation (no hand authoring)
Score each archetype from `ISO.styleOf(def)`: role affinity +3 (winger: technical, counter; striker: clinical; playmaker: possession, technical; target: aggressive, clinical; box2box: aggressive, counter; defender: defensive), trait affinity +2 each, attribute signals 0..2 (tck, skl, fin, pac, str, bal relative to ovr), plus a per-archetype BIAS that evens out coverage. Highest wins, ties by ARCH order. Then continuous nudges (about +-10%) from tck, skl, lng, fin, sho. Coverage over the 141 players: technical 30, clinical 31, aggressive 32, possession 15, counter 12, unpredictable 11, defensive 10 (7-23%).

## Harness (`tools/ai-test.js`, ~2 s smoke, ~2 min full; `--update` only for a deliberate sim change)
L legacy identity (18 hashes in `tools/ai-baseline.json`), A profiles (coverage 6-30%, caps, Prime, kill switches, create(diff,{pers:NEUTRAL}) == create(diff)), B 288 decision snapshots (12 scenes x 8 profiles x 3 difficulties, baseline `decisions`), C behaviour signatures (120 mirrored matches per archetype), D anti-stuck over every match (4720 in the full run), M win-rate matrix, E difficulty monotonicity (informational), T tuning aid (`ARCH=name`). CI runs `--smoke`.

## Results (full run, all gates pass)
Signatures vs neutral: aggressive tackle attempts per defending minute 1.31x; technical skills+stops per possession 1.57x; clinical mean shot distance 0.88x, goals per shot 0.91x; defensive opponent mean shot distance 1.11x, opponent goals 1.91 vs 2.02; counter mean gain-to-shot 0.80 s vs 1.05 s (0.76x); possession share 56.6%; unpredictable 16 mood switches per match, act entropy +0.055.
Matrix (row beats column, 20 bases x 4): see the run; every archetype within the field/neutral bands by 95% interval, widest pair clinical vs counter 66% (interval below the 65% gate). T (80 bases x 4 vs neutral): aggressive 50.6, technical 51.9, defensive 54.4, clinical 46.3, unpredictable 53.4, counter 58.4, possession ~48. Mean match 70 s neutral; no archetype above 1.5x. D: no NaN, no unfinished, no ball idle > 8 s, no 20 s hold, reversals 0.61% of play ticks, jockey toggles 0.75/s.
`sim.js 150 normal --pers`: 580 goals / 3140 shots / 1.11 min against 609 / 3002 / 1.08 (goals -4.8%, within 10%).

## Deviations and findings
- Gain-to-shot uses the mean and only balls won more than 190 px from the goal (the pitch is 340 px deep, the median was 0.8 s for both sides); counter needed a 230 px shoot-now band to reach 0.8x.
- Defensive: shots inside 150 px per possession did not move (the metric is noisy and a defence that wins the ball sooner leaves the attacker fewer minutes); replaced by opponent mean shot distance >= 1.08x and opponent goals <= 1.05x neutral. Clinical goals per shot gate is >= 0.9x neutral (sampling noise at 120 matches).
- Section C uses 30 bases x 4 (120 matches), not 40, to cut noise. M gates on 95% intervals, as `balance.js` does.
- E is not gated: legacy difficulty is not monotonic in CPU-vs-CPU play (neutral challenger vs normal: easy 54%, normal 43%, hard 43%). That is the old brain, unchanged; human-vs-CPU difficulty was not play-tested and nobody has played the personalities.
- `check.js 60` now reports one violation, 'profile: old shape not back-filled' (the Phase 2.5 profile schema check); it is not from the AI.

## Classification and blocked
2v2 CPU tactics (3.3) are BLOCKED on an offline 2v2 mode (4.5). 'Formations' do not apply to the one-goal 1v1 format; 3.3 here is pressing, drop-deep and counter play in 1v1.

# Phase 3 session 2: adaptive difficulty (3.2), keeper personalities (3.4), tactics remainder (3.3), decision-quality tests (3.5)

Classification: `ai.js`, `ISO.AI.Adapt`, the profile fields and the UI are offline only. The keeper style hooks are in sim-shared code (`js/game.js` `updateKeeper`, `keeperHold`, `distribute`) but neutral by default (every factor exactly 1, resolved from the keeper def at call time, nothing stored on G, keepers or players); only offline Quick Match and MyCareer pass a styled keeper, never online, drills or the menu demo (the def is not serialised, so a styled keeper online would diverge). Tripwires unchanged: ai-test L/S, `sim.js 40/150 normal`, `check.js 60`, `balance --smoke`, `anim-gate`, `test-determinism`, `bench-bandwidth` all byte-identical.

## Adaptive difficulty
`ISO.AI.LVL`, `paramsAt(lvl)` (piecewise-linear over DIFFS, the DIFFS object itself at integers), `create(diff, { pers, lvl, adapt })`, `ISO.AI.Adapt { fresh, sanitize, perf, next, level, keeperNerf, payout, label }`, `ADAPT = { MAX .5, STEP .15, DEAD .15, EMA .5, MOM .05 }`. perf = clamp(0.6*(gf-ga)/win + 0.25*(2*poss-1) + 0.15*(2*sot-1), -1, 1); ema smoothing, dead zone, off += STEP*ema, bounds: level in [0,2], |off| <= 0.5 (easy only harder, hard only easier). In-match momentum (opts.adapt): at each rep change the level is re-derived from the score (stateless, inside the tier band). Keeper nerf (16, 0, -6) and win payout (.4, .7, 1) follow the level; `hardWins` needs level >= 2. Profile: `P.adapt = { v, off, ema, n }` (back-filled and sanitised in migrate, keyed on shape: no schema bump), `settings.adaptive` (default true; off = exact tier), `adaptLevel(diff)`, `resetAdapt()`. UI: select screen line, career opponent line, end screen 'NEXT MATCH', Settings toggle and reset. Scope: Quick Match and MyCareer fixtures (not cups, drills, demo, online).
Harness F: next pure; 10,000 seeded sequences keep the bounds (max |off| 0.500); opt-out hashes equal legacy; corrupt state sanitised; payout/nerf monotone; 5 simulated humans at fixed level 0.6 x 30 adapted matches from Normal: 50.0% win rate, final levels 0.77..1.43, max |offset| 0.43. Five levels vs the fixed normal bot: 49 / 54 / 50 / 51 / 41% (NOT monotone: level 2, the Hard parameters, loses to Normal in CPU-vs-CPU play; legacy behaviour, reported not gated).

## Keeper personalities

Hooks: depth, narrow, rushP/rushR/rushCd, slideP/slideR, claim, antic, react, wait, readErr, lineSpd, hold, distDepth/distWide, penGuess (penalty drill only, guarded draw). Deviations: reflex depth 0.8 (spec 0.6; 0.6 cost 5.8 save-rate points); rushR is bounded by the box (inBox), so sweeper's 1.3 adds nothing: rush engagements aggressive 286 > sweeper 265 > neutral 233 > conservative 157 (spec asked sweeper > aggressive); reaction onset (reactT s): positioning .1307 < reflex .1365 < neutral .1397 < sweeper .1473 (positioning antic 1.3 beats reflex react .9 with antic .6; the shortest possible tap already charges, so there is no antic-free shot). Others as designed: depth at 300 px sweeper 29.5, positioning 28.3 > neutral 26.7 > reflex 24.4, conservative 23.5; claim rate sweeper 1.00 > aggressive .90 > positioning .79 > neutral .78; hold time .54 < .63 < .91 < 1.08; throw distance sweeper 310 > neutral 296, conservative widest (139 vs 116); penalty early commit aggressive 57%, others 0. 150 matches per style: goals per match 3.84-4.02 vs neutral 3.91 (within 8%), save rate (1 - goals per shot on target) 71-77% vs 75.9% (within 5 points).
