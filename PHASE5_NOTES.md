# Phase 5 session 1: 5.1 Career seasons

Skipped by the user: 1.5, 2.1, 4.5, 4.6, Phase 7, Phase 8. `ISO.SIM_REV` and the sim are untouched (default path byte-identical: `sim.js 40 normal`, `check.js 60`, `ai-test.js --smoke` diffed before and after).

## Design

- `js/career.js` (pure, no DOM, no `Math.random`; uses `ISO.Cup.prng/mix`). `ISO.SEASONS`: Break Through (OVR <= 76; CPU easy x4 then normal x3; win 10 pts; title 1000 cr; cap 75; no relegation), First Team (74-86; easy, normal x6; 14; 1800; 82), Elite (86-89; normal x4, hard x3; 18; 2600; 88), Legends (90-92, era legend; normal, hard x6; 22; 3600; 94), The Greatest (93+; hard x7; 26; 6000; 99). A loss gives 3 points. Relegation (7th-8th) is a data column (`rel`), null in Season 1. `cap` is data for 5.2, not enforced yet.
- A season is eight teams (you and seven rivals), seven matchdays, circle-method fixtures. The rivals are drawn by seed without replacement, sorted by OVR and slotted so you meet them weakest to strongest. Stored state: `{ i, n, field[7], md, res[], live }`. Other teams' results are derived from `mix(seed, i, n, matchday, fixture)` with the cup roll formula (copied, not imported-edited), never stored. No draws: a win is 3 league points, so a matchday hands out 12 and `pts = 3 * w` (asserted).
- Finish: 1st champion (prize 100%), 2nd-3rd up (40%), 4th-6th stay (10%), 7th-8th down from Season 2 (0%, Season 1 stays). Prize halved when you already won that season (`best[i] === 1`). Bonus points on closing the season: 2x win points for the title, 1x for promotion. The Season 5 champion stays at 5 (title defence) and sets `stats.careerDone`. `n` goes up by one every season so a replayed season is a new draw.
- Thin band: if fewer than 7 players fit, the band widens 1 OVR point per step (era after 20 steps). The shipped roster never needs it (smallest band has 15).
- Profile: `career.seed` (one uint32), `sn`, `hist` (cap 20), `best`; schema 3 -> 4. `career.stage` and `career.ladder` are frozen and kept for older builds. New stats `seasonsWon`, `promotions`. A career nobody has touched takes a clock seed once from the UI (`careerBegin`); migrated old saves get `seedOf` (FNV of name, stage, points, credits, matches).

## Decisions

- Quitting or reloading mid-fixture is a 0-3 loss (`live` flag written and saved at kickoff, cleared by the result; the forfeit counts a matchday, no retries). Restart is disabled in career matches, the pause menu says FORFEIT MATCH.
- Legacy saves start at the season their stage matches (0-5, 6-11, 12-14, 15-17, 18+ -> seasons 1-5), earlier seasons are logged as `legacy` ("CLEARED ON THE LADDER"); no retroactive prizes. Attributes, points, name and look are never touched. A season state that fails `valid()` is rebuilt at the same season.
- Achievements: families `seasons` (1 / 3 / 5 titles; saka, kane) and `promo` (1 / 4 promotions; kvara); `career_all` keeps its id and now says "Win Season 5". Box requirement ids unchanged.

## Measured (tools/season-test.js, 200 seeds)

- 0.5 bot promoted from Season 1: about 34.8% (1000 seeds), so the assertion band is 30-65% (the roadmap's 35% lower edge sits on the measured value). 0.7 bot reaches the Season 5 title within 12 seasons: 95 of 100 (mean 7.6 seasons). A perfect run earns 810 upgrade points over the five titles (roadmap target 700-800, legacy ~790; assertion 700-850) and 15,000 credits of prizes. Largest single prize 6000.
- `--sim`: seven real CPU-vs-CPU fixtures on the real sim with a `careerDef`-shaped def, no NaN, none unfinished, about 270 ms in total.

The #career screen (banner, tabs, table rows, summary card at 1280x720), the end screen line (`MATCHDAY n/7 · 3RD`), the forfeit toast, the CLOSE SEASON flow, the Play card and menu stats text.

# Phase 5 session 2: 5.2 Training and development, 5.3 Career objectives, 5.4 Reputation

Sim untouched (default path byte-identical: `sim.js 40 normal`, `check.js 60` output, `ai-test.js --smoke` diffed before and after; `ISO.SIM_REV` unchanged). Adaptive difficulty (Phase 3.2) is not duplicated: it keeps scaling the CPU, this session only pays and tracks what you do against it.

## Files
New `js/career-obj.js` (pure, loaded after `career.js`, extends `ISO.Career`; also in the node require lists of `check.js` / `season-test.js`). `js/profile.js` (schema 5, `normCareer` calls `ISO.Career.norm`, `careerSettle`, `careerObjectives`, `seasonObjectives`, `setFocus`, `trainStart/Finish/Abort`, floor in `setAttr`, kit / look cycling by tier, `seasonEnd` pays the season objectives), `js/career-ui.js` (OBJECTIVES and TRAINING tabs, reputation bar, hooks), one-line hooks in `js/main.js` (beginMatch, event loop, showEnd x3, showDrillEnd, setPaused, `career-drill` action), `index.html` (script tag, two tabs, `#careerObj`, `#pObj`), `css/style.css` (`/* Phase 5.2-5.4 */`), tests.

## Tracker (`ISO.Career.Tracker.create(team) / onEvent(tr, G, e) / finish(tr, G, { gap })`)
Reads only what `main.js` already drains from `G.events` plus `G.stats` (never writes G; tested by hashing G around `onEvent`). One line in the event loop. Goal replays do not re-emit events (the replay is the renderer's tape; only `Game.step` pushes events). A goal belongs to the last `kick` event (shot) of the scorer's team, which carries `weak`, the shot type, and the striker; outside the box comes from `G.ball.shot.out` when the ball still carries that shot, else from the striker's position with `C.BOXD/BOXY0/BOXY1/FW`. Summary `m`: won, gf, ga, time, poss %, shots, sot, saves, clears, power, skills, skillsOk (skills minus botches), tackles, goalsOut, goalsWeak, goalsByType, lost (a tackle or steal on you), botches, bursts, bumps, beats (`beaten` by you), comeback (won after trailing), ovrGap (opponent minus you), rating (0-10, the career's own formula, not the presentation one). Assists are not tracked (no assist event in the sim; the roadmap lists them, not built).

## Objectives (5.3)
`ISO.Career.OBJ` (23): easy (goal, 3 skill moves, 2 tackles, 3 shots on target, 45% possession, win), medium (**score two goals**, **five successful skill moves**, **score outside the box**, **score with the weak foot**, 55% possession, 5 tackles, power goal, dribble past 8 challenges), hard (**win without conceding**, dispossessed 5 times or fewer, **beat a stronger opponent** (only drawn when the gap is at least 2), win after going behind, plus rep-gated: outside-the-box and weak-foot in one match (rep 300), 60% possession and five skills (300), 3-0 with 55% (700), rating 9.0 (700), never dispossessed (1500)). Three per fixture, one per tier, a pure function of seed, season, attempt, matchday (`draw`); remembered in `career.obj` so a reload shows the same three; `n` caps repeats within a season; `minSeason` / `minRep` gate the pool. Rewards 40 / 80 / 150 credits x (1 + 0.25 season index), 5 / 10 / 20 reputation, 0 / 1 / 2 upgrade points. Season objectives (`SEASON_OBJ`, 14, three per season, one per tier, `seasonObjs(seed, i, n)`) are tallied in `sn.tally` and paid in `seasonEnd`: 100 / 200 / 300 credits x the same multiplier, 15 / 30 / 60 rep, 1 / 2 / 3 points. UI: next-fixture list in the career right column, OBJECTIVES tab (this fixture, this season with the tally, reputation tiers), pause overlay (live ticks; result-dependent ones stay blank until the end), end-screen rows (`OBJECTIVES 2/3 ... ✓ / ✗`), season objectives in the close-season summary and toast. Achievement family `objDone` (1 / 10 / 40 / 100).
Deviation: "complete a match without losing possession" is "dispossessed 5 times or fewer" (the CPU averages 10 per match, so zero is 1%); the literal version exists as `o_never`, rep 1500.

## Training (5.2)
`ISO.Career.TRAIN`: sprint (pac, acc), dribbling (dri, agi), finishing (fin, sho), strength (str, bal), technical (bct, skl), endurance (sta). XP per fixture from `measure(m)` (capped at 100 per programme, doubled on `career.focus`). Tokens: one per played fixture, bank of 2, spent at drill kickoff; finishing uses target / penalty / power / oneonone, technical finesse / freekick / chip, dribbling oneonone, sprint / strength / endurance are match-only (`drills` is data, 4.3 will fill it). The drill is played as the career player (`career-drill` action sets `App.pick`; the drill's score over its max is the XP, 100 at most; a drill left unfinished loses the token and pays nothing). Gains: +1 attribute per `100 * upgradeCost(v)` XP, lowest attribute of the programme first, never above `SEASONS[i].cap` (training only; upgrade points still reach 99); XP at the cap is banked (up to 600) and released when the next season raises the cap. `career.trained[k]` counts trained points and `setAttr`'s floor is `ATTR_MIN + trained[k]`, so trained points cannot be refunded. A trait unlock toasts once (`career.traits` remembers the set). XP scales (`measure`) were set from CPU-vs-CPU averages of the real sim (80 matches: 8.4 tackles, 8.3 shots on target, 1.0 bursts, 1.7 skills, 0.4 bumps, 8.4 beats, 70 s) with skills and bursts assumed about double for a human; a human playtest should recheck them.
Budget: XP / 100 is worth the same upgrade points at any level, so the share is simply trained points over all growth. `season-test.js` perfect run (35 fixtures, a token drill each fixture at 55% of the max): training 13.6% of career growth (138 point-equivalents, 126 levels) against 878 upgrade points; assertion band 10-15%.

## Reputation (5.4)
`career.rep` only goes up. Per fixture: win 8, the OVR gap of a stronger opponent beaten (up to 10), win streak 2 per extra win (up to 10), goals (up to 6), rating above 6 x 2, one per three successful skills (up to 3), plus the objectives' rep; a season finish pays 60 champion / 30 up / 10 stay / 0 down (+10 per season index for champion or up). Tiers at 0 / 100 / 300 / 700 / 1500 / 3000 / 5000: ROOKIE, PROSPECT, RISING STAR, FIRST CHOICE, STAR, SUPERSTAR, LEGEND. Unlocks: four kits and four looks appended at the END of `CAREER_KITS` / `CAREER_LOOKS` (indices 6-9; tiers 2, 3, 4, 5; `KIT_REQ` / `LOOK_REQ`, the arrows skip locked ones), the rep-gated objectives, rival challenges (`RIVAL_CH`, data only for session 3). Achievement family `careerRep` (300 / 1500 / 5000). The perfect synthetic run ends at about 2,700 rep (STAR); SUPERSTAR needs more than one career.

## Profile (schema 5, keyed on shape)
`career.rep, xp{6}, trained{}, tokens, focus, traits (null until first settle), obj, streak`, `sn.tally`, `stats.objDone`. `ISO.Career.norm` back-fills and repairs them (unknown programme / attribute ids dropped, tokens 0-2, rep never negative; a session-1 career gets rep 0, one token, focus finishing); idempotent. `recordMatch` (career) takes the tracker summary as `r.m` and pays objectives / XP / token / rep; **without `r.m` it pays none of them** (old callers, tests). A forfeit or a reload mid-fixture pays nothing and resets the win streak.

## Economy
Objective credits for a whole career, every objective done: 18,675 (match objectives 14,175, season objectives 4,500), `OBJ_CREDIT_CAP` 19,000. With the 5.1 prize bound of 15,000 the ceiling is 34,000 credits over five seasons (asserted). Upgrade points from objectives: at most 21 per season (about 105 + 30 over a career) against the 810 of 5.1.

## Tests

## Rivals (5.5)
- Four fictional rivals (`RIVALS`): Rex Calder (winger, Unpredictable, objective: score from outside the box), Mateo Vantelli (striker, Clinical, concede one goal or fewer), Bruno Kessler (defender, Defensive, win with 55% possession), Sol Orozco (playmaker, Possession, win after going behind). Each has a look, kit, role, an `ISO.PROF` attribute shape, a forced `ISO.AI.archProfile` archetype and intro / win / loss / rematch lines. `rivalDef(r, level)` is pure: overall = `min(97, base + 7 * level)`, attributes = ISO.PROF row around it, a def clone with `base` (like `ISO.primeOf`), no shared references. Level = season index + earlier meetings (any result, forfeits included), so each rematch is harder.
- Which rival closes a season: `RIVALS[(mix(seed, 0xb055) + i + n) % 4]`, pure. The finale uses `bossDiff(i)`: the season's last CPU level raised one step (so Hard from Season 1 on), first to 5.
- State: `career.sn.boss = { id, done, live, res }` (per season, back-filled by shape, also on a season installed by `seasonEnd`), `career.rivals[id] = { w, l, gf, ga, met }` for all four, `career.h2h[playerId] = { w, l, gf, ga }` for ordinary season opponents (the 40 most recent, written by `recordMatch`, `careerForfeit` and the live-flag forfeit on load).
- The finale ("matchday 8") is **optional and outside the league**: after matchday 7 the right column shows the rival and PLAY FINALE; the season can be closed at any time, and closing skips it with no record. It never changes `sn.md`, `sn.res`, the table, upgrade points, XP, tokens or the streak (asserted). `startMatch` (main.js) sets `App.boss`, `App.matchPers = ISO.AI.archProfile(arch)` (used by `buildMatch` for the CPU seat only, career mode only, never quiet or online) and kicks off with `sn.boss.live = true`; the result goes through `recordMatch({ mode: 'boss', m })`. Quitting, reloading or `careerForfeit` while live is a loss on the ledger (gf 0, ga 3) and pays nothing; `seasonEnd` refuses to run while the finale is live.
- Rewards (win only): prize `[200, 350, 550, 800, 1200]` by season times the rematch factor (+20% per earlier win over that rival, at most +60%), reputation `20 + 5 * season + 5 per earlier win (max 3)`, plus three objectives (the rival's own 60 CR / 5 rep, win by three or more 80 / 8, match rating 7.0 or better 120 / 12) scaled by the season's 1 + 0.25 i. A loss pays only the ordinary match credits (`40 + 15 * goals`). The ordinary win bonuses of a win (`mode: 'boss'` is a plain match to the generic path: wins, credits) still apply.
- UI: RIVALS tab (record, level and overall of the next meeting, the line the rival will say, the objective, this season's finale marker, head to head), a finale row (matchday 8) on FIXTURES, H2H text on every fixture, a banner above CLOSE SEASON, the right-column rival card, the pause list (finale objectives), the intro card (`SEASON FINALE · NICKNAME · line`, CPU archetype chip from `App.matchPers`), the end screen (title RIVAL BEATEN, prize, objectives, rep, toasts). KIT tab: two colour inputs for the custom kit.
- `RIVAL_CH` (session 2 data) is not wired and stays data only.

## Collection (5.6)
- `ISO.Collection.SETS()`: 35 sets built from the roster (14 clubs and 14 nations with at least 3 players, 5 rarities, 2 eras). Credits: clubs and nations 25 per player + 150; rarity 30 per player; era 15 per player. Claim: `claimSet(id)` once per complete set (`P.sets[id] = true`, idempotent across reloads). Total of all sets 15,745 CR, asserted under `SET_CREDIT_CAP` 16,000 (it needs all 141 players).
- Set bonus: +5% per completed set the fielded player belongs to (ownership, claimed or not), capped +15%, as the 5th argument of `winMult` (`wm.set`): it multiplies the existing win-bonus factor in Quick Match (`mode: 'cpu'`, the fielded player is `r.me.base || r.me`). The select screen and the end screen show it (`SETS ×1.15`). Career and cup payouts are unchanged. The default `winMult(diff, me, opp, lvl)` results are identical to before.
- Duplicates: a duplicate box pull or achievement player keeps its credit refund and adds a star (`P.dups[id]`, max 3); `PF.primeCost(d)` = catalogue price times `1 - 0.1 * stars` (so at least 70%); `buyPrime` and the store button use it.
- Favourites: `P.favs`, at most 12, known ids only, deduplicated on load. Custom kit: `career.kitCustom = { a, b }`, lowercase `#rrggbb` only (anything else is refused or dropped on load), applied in `careerDef` (shirt, socks and trim `a`; shorts `b`), the def id changes so the sprite cache does not reuse the old kit.
- Screens (`collection-ui.js`): store COLLECTION tab (PLAYERS grid with ALL / OWNED / FAVOURITES filters, favourite heart, compare tick; SETS list with progress and CLAIM), the player card modal (attributes, role, traits with descriptions, weak foot and skill stars, CPU personality, celebration, duplicate stars and Prime price, set progress; opened from the grid, the PLAYER CARD button of the store's players tab, or right-click / shift-click on a card in the select grid and the store roster) and the comparison modal (two columns with the differences).
- **Custom stadiums: BLOCKED.** It needs new map-rendering work in both renderers (a stadium editor or a parameterised map), which this session does not fake. Recorded in ROADMAP.md.
